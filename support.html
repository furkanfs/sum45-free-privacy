How to play

Drag one numbered tile onto another to swap them. Match every row with the target number on its right and every column with the target number below it. Solve all six targets to complete the puzzle.

Audio or settings problems

Open Pause → Settings to adjust the master, music, and sound-effect volumes. Use Reset Audio if you want to restore the default volume settings.

Resetting local data

Sum45 Free stores progress and preferences only on your device. You can remove this local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. There is no online account or server-side copy.

Reporting a problem

Email Sum45 Freefree@gmail.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version, Sum45 Free version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful. Please do not send sensitive personal information.
